Cardwork Cycle is a deck-building farming game created for Ludum Dare 52. It is
pretty derivative of Slay the Spire mechanically (energy system + card rewards),
but it ended up being decently enjoyed by the Ludum Dare crowd and by some other
folks who I watched play it.

This game scored 25th overall and 9th for fun in the Jam (72hour team) category in Ludum Dare despite being a solo project.
