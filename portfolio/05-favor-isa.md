FaVOR is a custom instruction set architecture I designed for my Advanced Computer Architecture term project.

I designed the ISA, its binary encoding, and its calling convention. I implemented it in GNU binutils, which included parsing assembly language, handling assembly fixups, and implementing custom relocations.

Later, as a personal project, I implemented part of the ISA as an LLVM backend.
