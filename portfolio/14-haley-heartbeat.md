Haley Heartbeat is a (sort of) "bullet hell" game, designed along the lines of
a sort of puzzle game. In particular, each encounter with enemies can be
"defused" by fighting them in a certain order.

The game is also a rhythm game: tapping the fire button in a heartbeat pattern
gives you successively more powerful shots. The music is designed around this
rhythm and so has a sort of jaunty vibe.
